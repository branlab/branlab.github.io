Marilyn is a faculty member of BRAN Lab and Assistant Professor in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Northeastern University London. Her work sits at the intersection of multivariate information theory, statistics, machine learning and whole-brain modelling. She argues that pairwise connectivity misses much of the structure of brain function, and develops higher-order information-theoretic measures, such as O-information and synergy–redundancy decompositions, to capture it. She has applied these to healthy ageing, frontotemporal dementia and transcranial ultrasound stimulation.
