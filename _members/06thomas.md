Thomas is a PhD student at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon, co-supervised by Prof. Andreia Sofia Teixeira. He studies decentralised finance as an economic and network system, from how governance power is concentrated across DeFi protocols to the strategic economics of recovering bad debt in decentralised lending markets.
